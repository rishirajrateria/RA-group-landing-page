About our business

Built on vision, nationalism and philanthropy.

A visionary entrepreneur, a committed nationalist and a philanthropist, Haji Ramzan Ali helped pave the path to a business conglomerate from Eastern India and beyond by seeding pioneering businesses in different sectors. With diversification and induction of professionals to establish a venerable business of excellence, the Group proudly heads manufacturing companies in different sectors, construction and property development companies, export houses, and a charity organisation.

The foundation of the R.A. Group was laid before 1947. Like many young nationalists of the time, Haji Ramzan Ali left his job with a jute mill and started his own enterprise, R.A. Perfumery Works, from a small shop in Central Calcutta, still the Group’s flagship business today.
